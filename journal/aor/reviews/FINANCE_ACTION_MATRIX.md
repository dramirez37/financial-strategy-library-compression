## Priority order

1. **Finance-identity gate:** FIN-01, FIN-02, FIN-03, FIN-05.
2. **Economic-measurement gate:** FIN-04, FIN-06, FIN-07, FIN-09.
3. **Empirical-validity gate:** FIN-08, FIN-12.
4. **Special-issue positioning gate:** FIN-10, FIN-13, FIN-14, FIN-15.
5. **Reporting integrity:** FIN-11, FIN-16.

The smallest defensible revision is to relabel the finance studies as stylized,
retrospective mechanism audits and narrow the paper to its operations-research
content. The stronger revision requires new point-in-time financial evidence,
an empirically defensible module ontology, calibrated burden units, and an
instantiated research-generation process. Existing locked and historical
artifacts must remain unchanged; any new analysis requires a new prospective
design and provenance chain.
