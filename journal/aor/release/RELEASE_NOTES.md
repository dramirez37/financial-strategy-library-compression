# v0.2.0 AoOR submission release candidate

This release candidate packages the journal article *Innovation-Safe
Compression of Financial Strategy Libraries: Semantics, Complexity, and
Algorithms* and its independently compiled
Online Resource 1 for submission to *Annals of Operations Research*.

The release adds the identity-closure tagged-cover theorem, the restricted
NP-completeness result, the parameterized deletion-gap theorem, exact
preprocessing, enumeration and requirement-mask dynamic programming, weighted
greedy and certified deletion algorithms, auditable HiGHS MIP integration, the
registered Algorithmic Compression Benchmark v2, and the two retrospective
financial resource audits. Evidence classes remain distinct: solver status is
not formal proof, exact fixtures are not universal theorems, and retrospective
financial diagnostics are not causal, forecasting, alpha, or deployable-
performance claims.

The public benchmark archive includes unsuccessful runs in its registered
denominators and preserves the committed design, locks, registries, canonical
raw result records, solver logs, exact audit certificates, and manifest. The
immutable repository also retains process-control handshakes, but those files
contain machine-local workspace paths and are excluded from the redistributable
archive through an explicit export rule. The release does not rerun the final
benchmark.

Raw and row-level CRSP/WRDS observations are excluded. Only redistributable
aggregate financial outputs and public exact certificates are included. The
source and artifact licenses are recorded in `LICENSE`; third-party and
licensed-data exclusions remain in force.

The historical `release/v0.1.1-arxiv/` preprint release remains unchanged and
is not relabeled as the journal benchmark or journal submission.

Zenodo-compatible metadata are included, but no DOI has been assigned or is
claimed. This release candidate is not described as accepted, forthcoming, or
peer reviewed.
