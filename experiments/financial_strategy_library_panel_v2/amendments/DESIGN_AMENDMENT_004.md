# Financial panel v2 design amendment 004 — operational flagship arm

Two outcome-blind computational pilots established that exact MIP and 64-start compression cannot serve as a practical gate on a 20-origin population experiment, even after removing the per-security source quota and inline timing repetitions. Neither pilot serialized a predecision result or opened a held-out row.

The population question is therefore sharpened to an operational one: does deterministic innovation-safe weighted greedy compression, followed by exact-feasibility reverse deletion, improve the next-year utility of frozen masked choices relative to a frontier-only greedy library augmented under exactly the same realized burden cap? Both endpoints receive exact mandatory, frontier, closure, and rational burden checks. This comparison isolates closure for the executable workflow without claiming global minimum burden.

Exact MIP, frontier-only MIP, and 64-start deletion remain important algorithmic evidence but move to a separately run computational benchmark. They receive terminal `DEFERRED_TO_COMPUTATIONAL_BENCHMARK` rows in the population artifact and cannot affect menus, choices, availability, or held-out analysis.

The universe, full-calendar rule, shrinkage estimator, compact source dockets, scenarios, menus, and availability gates are unchanged. The primary arm changes, so every prior predecision run is excluded and restart 003 uses another disjoint registered seed file. No postdecision outcome informed this amendment.
