\subsection{Interpretation limits and future design}

The structured generator requested cells that were mathematically incompatible
with its incidence constraints. Those failures account for
\AORGenerationFailed\ terminal records from
\AORGenerationFailedInstances\ source-instance cells and are retained in every
denominator.
Among generated structured instances, fixed-point preprocessing commonly
selected all forced carriers and left an empty residual cover. Family B
therefore documents the prevalence and consequences of that preprocessing
mechanism in this design; it is weak evidence about residual MIP or dynamic-
programming search difficulty.

All recorded times include cold Julia startup and compilation within each
subprocess, one observation per run unit, eight-worker contention, and a dirty
worktree recorded in the execution metadata. They are execution records, not
steady-state speed estimates or a basis for close algorithm rankings. A
performance profile is not interpreted because feasibility heuristics, exact
methods, and time-limited solvers do not share one registered success target.

A defensible follow-up requires its own registry, seeds, and lock. It should
warm algorithms outside timed regions; block repeated clean-tree runs by
instance; validate generator feasibility before locking; balance realized
nonempty residual structure; define separate feasibility, quality-qualified,
and exact-completion targets; and include a bounded certified-exchange method
and LP-relaxation diagnostic. These are prospective design requirements, not
post hoc additions to v2.

The full coefficients, paired comparisons, heuristic-quality summaries,
adversarial tables, plot-data tables, row trace, and SVGs remain generated
inputs under \path{journal/aor/tables/} and \path{journal/aor/figures/}. The
source-to-output map is
\path{journal/aor/tables/ANALYSIS_ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.csv}. The main article uses
a compact selection of those inputs; this resource preserves the complete
computational records without reproducing the main article's narrative.

The benchmark is synthetic and identity-closure only. It is not a probability
sample of financial libraries and supports no population, causal, forecasting,
alpha, or deployable-performance claim.
