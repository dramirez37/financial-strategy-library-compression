# Financial Evidence Provenance

## Publication-facing evidence layers

1. **Point-in-Time Portfolio-Library Retention Study** — retrospective
   licensed-data evidence and the article's financial case study.
2. **Calibrated Closure-Option Mechanism Experiment** — design-locked
   synthetic mechanism evidence using public-safe aggregate nuisance
   calibration.

The two layers are intentionally complementary. The retrospective study shows
large structural burden reduction and option creation but no common-equity
option exercise and one adverse ETF exercise. The calibrated experiment shows
that a small positive bridge can be difficult for a conservative learner to
recover while a prospectively powered bridge is recovered.

## Immutable internal identifiers

The repository directories and experiment IDs retain their original versioned
names because those strings appear in design locks, result seals, source hashes,
and deterministic reproduction commands. Renaming those paths would invalidate
provenance without changing the science. Version labels are therefore internal
implementation identifiers only; they do not appear as study names in the
article or Online Resource.

## Superseded financial evidence

Earlier terminal, annual, and intermediate panel experiments remain available
as historical repository artifacts. They are not active manuscript inputs and
must not be described as part of the point-in-time study or calibrated
mechanism experiment.

## Licensed-data boundary

No raw CRSP/WRDS row, security identifier, or return path is redistributed.
Public tables are generated only from sealed aggregate manifests that declare
the licensed-data exclusions. Reproduction of the source-data stages requires
independent licensed access; aggregate table regeneration and seal checking do
not.

One predecision audit receipt records machine-specific Julia and native HiGHS
paths. It therefore remains local rather than entering the public tree. The
committed predecision result seal records its SHA-256 digest, so the executed
receipt remains bound to the scientific chain without publishing a local home
directory. This exclusion contains no result choice and changes no design,
seed, policy, or outcome.
