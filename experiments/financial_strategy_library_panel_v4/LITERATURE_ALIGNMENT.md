# Literature and AOR alignment

The experiment follows the paper's sharp frontier-only relaxation loss and
joint descendant-event lower bound. Strict positivity is registered through
the net bridge margin rather than inferred from the largest historical
candidate return.

The design addresses data-snooping concerns by using one lineage-designated
target descendant, disjoint calibration and test seeds, an independently
calibrated null threshold, a complete ledger, and immutable null/adversarial
controls. V3's White Reality Check, Hansen SPA, Deflated Sharpe, and PBO
diagnostics remain relevant external evidence explaining why the 96-candidate
historical maximum is not promoted to the primary v4 treatment.

The experiment is a mechanism validation study. Market data calibrate nuisance
noise and dependence; the theory supplies the sign of the treatment margin.
That division is disclosed so a positive result cannot be confused with an
empirical return discovery.
