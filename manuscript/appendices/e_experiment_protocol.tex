\section{Experiment Design and Information Set}\label{app:protocol}

\subsection{Registered randomized design}\label{app:protocol-randomized}

The registered synthetic study fixes \(N=1024\) horizon-four trials in a
complete \(2^7\) factorial with eight replications in each of 128 cells.  The
master seed and all derived trial seeds were fixed before outcomes.  Profiles,
kernels, project laws, losses, and values use exact
\texttt{Rational\{BigInt\}} arithmetic.

Raw realizability is a failure gate, not a post hoc filter.  Each interaction
observation must be generated from four realizable raw-corner libraries in one
catalog and module system.  Every enumerated compressed state must have a raw
witness, and raw and compressed values must agree exactly at every registered
belief and remaining horizon.  The design also requires exact frontier,
closure, operational, generative, total-loss, and decomposition agreement
under innovation-safe pruning.  The experiment is a synthetic structural
stress test of the registered finite generator, not a population sample or a
population-frequency estimate.  Full estimand equations, response grids,
factor slices, sequential diagnostics, and the frozen pilot are in Online
Supplement~S5.

\subsection{Financial information set}\label{app:protocol-financial}

The terminal financial audit uses one fixed development--validation--holdout
split.  The annual walk-forward financial audit uses five target-year
decisions, each based on information through the preceding year.  Their
held-out outcomes and resampling units differ, so the two protocols are
defined and reported separately throughout the paper and supplement.

The validation frontier is known at pruning time, and structural closure is
known at the same time.  Held-out descendant quality is first read only after
pruning is fixed.  For annual target year \(y\), scores and selected sets use
information only through \(y-1\); target-year outcomes remain held out until
after the decision hash is fixed.  The greedy oracle uses target-year outcomes
and is therefore infeasible.  No held-out quantity enters either compression
rule.

\subsection{Data and optimization qualifications}
\label{app:protocol-qualifications}

Both financial audits use a licensed CRSP/WRDS source.  Raw and row-level data
are nonredistributable.  The ETF universes are retrospective and are not
survivorship-free; neither audit is prospective.

For the reported mixed-integer searches, \texttt{OPTIMAL} is the status
reported by HiGHS and is numerical global-search evidence, not an exact
enumeration certificate.  Every displayed selected library, integer burden,
frontier, and closure is rechecked exactly after the solve.  The financial
audits support no causal, forecasting, alpha, or deployable-performance claim.
Full financial formulas, secondary results, resampling details, and the mixed
or adverse outcomes are in Online Supplement~S6; configuration histories,
hashes, amendments, and artifact paths are in Online Supplement~S7.
