\subsection{Bridge-margin sensitivity}

The bridge comparison also gives one useful sensitivity warning.  Its gross
descendant value is \(A_{\mathrm{br}}=\beta^d\rho^d\pi C\), its signed net
margin is \(M_{\mathrm{br}}=A_{\mathrm{br}}-\kappa\), and its normalized
positive margin is \(m_{\mathrm{br}}=M_{\mathrm{br}}/A_{\mathrm{br}}\).
On the positive-value branch, named-coordinate elasticities are amplified by
\(1/m_{\mathrm{br}}\), so the bridge becomes locally fragile as its normalized
margin approaches zero.  Vanishing value scale alone is not enough, and at or
below the threshold ordinary percentage elasticity is undefined.  Appendix~C
gives the core sensitivity formulas and switching conventions; extended
belief-occupation, coverage, persistence, and frontier--closure interaction
results are in Online Supplements~S1--S3.
