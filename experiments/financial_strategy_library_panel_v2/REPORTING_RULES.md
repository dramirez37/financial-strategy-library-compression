# Financial Innovation-Challenge Panel v2 — reporting rules

1. Use the full study title and `financial-strategy-library-panel-v2`.
2. State that all v1 outcomes were known before v2 was designed.
3. Call v2 prospective only relative to its transformations, menus, arms, and
   analysis—not prospectively collected data.
4. Never pool v1 and v2.
5. Report all 20 origins and every availability reason.
6. Keep focused and broad dockets separate before a cross-docket summary.
7. Lead with the budget-matched safe-versus-frontier-only contrast, not the
   source compression percentage.
8. Report comparator slack explicitly; do not describe a shared budget cap as
   exact equality of realized burden.
9. Describe top-choice availability as a mechanical implication of declared
   closure; describe next-year score differences as retrospective empirical
   outcomes that may have either sign.
10. Do not call a masked candidate a realized institutional innovation.
11. Do not interpret burden units as dollars, labor hours, headcount, or
    population resource savings.
12. Report the 30 scenario rows and exact frontier certificates before any
    held-out outcome.
13. Report whether the primary availability gate passed before the primary
    point estimate.
14. If the gate fails, use the registered word `inconclusive`; do not repair
    the analysis post hoc.
15. Do not substitute a different menu candidate after postdecision data are
    opened.
16. Report delisting-to-cash handling and unresolved return counts.
17. Separate population-block prevalence from the conditional computational
    stress block.
18. Do not mix feasible construction, quality-qualified attainment, and exact
    completion in one performance profile.
19. Timing claims must say warm steady state and identify the timed scope.
20. Solver `OPTIMAL` is solver evidence plus exact incumbent checks, not formal
    or exhaustive proof.
21. Make no causal, forecasting, alpha, deployable-performance,
    population-savings, or observed-institutional-cost claim.
22. Public outputs may contain audited origin-level aggregates, hashes,
    certificates, and synthetic replay rows; they may not contain raw CRSP rows,
    security identifiers, or row-level licensed return derivatives.
23. The manuscript must report a null or negative primary contrast with the
    same prominence as a positive contrast.
