dominance, rare carriers, symmetry, and bundles. The instance registry fixes

\subsection{Methods and evidence}

The method grid includes enumeration, mask dynamic programming, two registered
HiGHS formulations, weighted and cardinality greedy, greedy followed by reverse
deletion, deterministic deletion orders, seeded random deletion, and 32-start
deletion. Maximum immediate release and heaviest-safe-first are identical
under additive weights and constitute one scientific method. The record keeps
the two registered labels so that run-unit counts remain reproducible.

The independent audit checks original-coordinate mandatory retention, tagged
coverage, complete frontier and closure equality, and exact burden. It accepts
\AORBenchmarkAccepted\ libraries among \AORBenchmarkRuns\ terminal run units.
All \AORBenchmarkUnsuccessful\ unsuccessful units remain in the denominator.
Of \AORExactCapable\ exact-capable instances, \AORExactAgreement\ have all
required exact-method burdens available and equal, and \AORExactUnavailable\
have incomplete comparisons. Equal burdens with different optimizer identities
occur in \AOROptimizerIdentityDifferences\ comparisons and are consistent with
ties. Medium structured references are exactly rechecked HiGHS incumbents;
solver-reported global optimality is distinct from the exact feasibility audit.

\subsection{Interpretation and execution conditions}

Full preprocessing commonly resolves structured instances completely. This
supports the importance of forced carriers in this design but leaves limited
evidence about difficult residual search. Multi-start completes all starts on
\AORMultistartComplete\ instances and improves on its first start on
\AORMultistartImproved; successful sensitivity runs do not replace failed
primary runs.

Execution used Julia \AORJuliaVersion\ and HiGHS \AORHiGHSVersion\ on an
\AORCPU, with \AORWorkerCount\ concurrent one-thread processes. Times include
compilation and worker contention, and the execution record identifies the
source worktree. They are not steady-state speed estimates. The registered
performance profile uses unlike success targets across methods. The registered
HC3 models also omit within-instance dependence and cannot recover missing
generator cells. Neither output supports the article's comparative or
inferential claims. The published comparison instead uses exact agreement,
certified burden, preprocessing, and the complete status census.

The data files named in the reproduction manifest supply the complete
instance registry, exact-agreement records, method-level burden summaries,
preprocessing and multi-start summaries, and terminal records. The PDF reports
aggregates; no source row is removed from those current study records.
