\title[Innovation-safe strategy-library compression]{Innovation-Safe Compression of Financial Strategy Libraries: Semantics, Complexity, and Algorithms}
\author*[1]{\fnm{David} \sur{Ramirez}}\email{\AORCorrespondingEmail}
\affil*[1]{\orgname{\AORAffiliationOrganization},
\orgaddress{\city{\AORAffiliationCity}, \state{\AORAffiliationState}, \country{\AORAffiliationCountry}};
\href{https://orcid.org/\AORCorrespondingORCID}{ORCID: \AORCorrespondingORCID}}
\abstract{A portfolio research team can reduce maintenance burden by retiring strategies, but an entry that adds nothing to today's trading menu may still enable valuable future research. We ask which entries can be removed while preserving both uses. We give a conditional preservation principle: an operating frontier and a declared closure of reusable capabilities preserve the controlled research and operating process under explicit invariance assumptions. With identity closure, minimum-burden safe retention is exactly classical weighted set cover. This representation supplies preprocessing rules, exact methods, and a transferred greedy guarantee. Counterexamples show that preserving current operation can destroy future research value and that heaviest-safe-first deletion can be arbitrarily far from minimum burden. The computational benchmark retains all 3,907 run outcomes and verifies exact-method agreement wherever the required comparisons are available. A retrospective financial study finds large burden-index reductions and additional research options, but no policy replacement in common equities and a loss from its sole ETF replacement. A calibrated synthetic experiment distinguishes option availability from reliable exercise: a small positive bridge value is not recovered, while a prospectively powered value is. These results establish a specified retention principle and bounded experimental findings, without establishing a market premium.}
\keywords{financial strategy libraries, innovation-safe compression, weighted set cover, exact algorithms, model governance}
