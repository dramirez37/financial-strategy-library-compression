\abstract{\textbf{DRAFT scaffold abstract.}
Financial strategy libraries impose validation, monitoring, computing, and
governance burdens, while retained strategies can support both current
operation and the generation of future strategies. This article studies a
finite, source-relative retention problem under partial information. Under the
declared process, the operating frontier and generative closure form the
productive library state. Innovation-safe compression seeks a minimum-burden
sublibrary that retains the inactive strategy and preserves both objects
exactly. The journal version will connect this semantic reduction to a tagged
weighted covering representation under identity closure, its computational
complexity, and a portfolio of exact, mixed-integer, approximation-guaranteed,
and certified-deletion methods. Universal mathematical claims will be
distinguished from Lean kernel verification, exact finite computation, solver
diagnostics, and synthetic evidence. A registered algorithmic benchmark and
two retrospective financial library audits will provide separate
computational and application evidence using only committed, auditable
artifacts. Licensed CRSP/WRDS rows will not be redistributed, and held-out
financial quantities will be used only after retention decisions. The study
makes no causal, forecasting, alpha, or deployable-performance claim. This
scaffold fixes the submission architecture and evidence boundaries; it does
not yet constitute the full journal prose.}
