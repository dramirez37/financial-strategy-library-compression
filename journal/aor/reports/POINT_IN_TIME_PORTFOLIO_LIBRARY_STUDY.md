# Point-in-Time Portfolio-Library Retention Study

## Journal disposition

This is the article's primary retrospective financial study. It replaces the
earlier terminal and annual tuple-grammar cases in the active manuscript. The
historical cases remain repository provenance but are not active article or
Online Resource inputs.

The study is a transparent structural success and financial null:

- all gate-passed innovation-safe libraries retain the mandatory inactive
  strategy and exactly reproduce the declared source frontier, identity
  closure, and integer burden under independent rechecks;
- mean source-burden reduction is 73.1% across 19 common-equity origins and
  74.7% across 18 gate-passed ETF origins;
- the safe libraries expose about 66 proposal actions per origin that are
  absent from their budget-matched frontier-only comparators;
- the protected policy never replaces the common-equity comparator, so all
  common-equity held-out contrasts are mechanically zero; and
- one ETF replacement realizes a held-out certainty-equivalent difference of
  -0.038958, producing a mean of -0.002164 across the 18 complete ETF origins.

The manuscript must not turn the structural option count into a performance
claim or hide the adverse ETF exercise.

## Design identity

For origin year `y`, formation uses `y-5:y-3`, compression uses `y-2:y`,
proposal and policy freezing use `y+1`, and evaluation uses `y+2`. Point-in-time
eligibility does not require future survival. Observed delisting returns remain
in the licensed workflow, and the failed 2005 ETF gate remains in all
registered denominators.

The strategy object is a diversified portfolio specification applied to one
contemporaneous universe. Closure contains 31 rule, interface, data,
corporate-action, portfolio-aggregation, and cost/capacity capabilities. Cash
is always feasible. The safe policy inherits the exact comparator choice and
may replace it only when a simultaneous moving-block lower bound clears the
registered hurdle.

## Evidence status

Three gate-passed safe cells are resolved by exact finite preprocessing. The
other 34 carry HiGHS `OPTIMAL` status. All 37 pass exact original-instance
feasibility and burden rechecks. The rechecks certify feasibility and burden;
the global-optimality claim for the 34 MIP cells remains solver evidence, not
formal or exhaustive proof.

The public evaluation result contains neither raw returns nor licensed
security identifiers. Its fixed-choice replay audit passed, and every
unavailable or failed row remains represented.

## Claim boundary

Permitted claims concern the finite audited libraries, declared burden index,
option coverage, protected proposal decisions, and reported held-out
contrasts. The study does not establish population savings, causal effects,
forecasting skill, alpha, deployable profitability, or the completeness of an
institution's undocumented capability inventory.
