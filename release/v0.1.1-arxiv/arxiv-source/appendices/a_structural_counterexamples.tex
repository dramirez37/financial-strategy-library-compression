\subsection{Essential assumption-boundary examples}
\label{app:structural-counterexamples}

These are the two structural failures needed to delimit the headline
projection and characterization results.  Auxiliary finite-model examples
are in Online Supplement~S3.

\begin{counterexample}[Raw identifiers defeat frontier--closure sufficiency]
\label{cx:raw-identifier-generator}
There are exact finite libraries with the same frontier and closure but
different candidate laws when a broader generator is allowed to inspect
membership of a hidden raw strategy identifier.  With unit admission the
hidden identifier generates a module-bearing candidate, so the exact
next-compressed-state laws also differ.  That generator fails the raw
frontier--closure factorization imposed by the unified raw model.
\end{counterexample}

\begin{counterexample}[Behaviorally invisible raw closure]
\label{cx:raw-closure-invisible}
There is an exact finite raw process whose project menu is empty at every
state.  Two libraries have the same frontier and different closures but agree
on all five unified dynamic-innovation observations.  The process fails raw
closure detectability, so the characterization's converse cannot omit that
assumption.
\end{counterexample}
