\section{Solver Diagnostics and Independent Exactness Records}
\label{sec:or-solver-exactness}

The baseline binary covering model fixes mandatory columns, places one
at-least-one constraint on every residual tagged row, and minimizes the exact
weighted burden after a verified integer scaling. HiGHS runs record solver and
wrapper versions, deterministic controls, time limit, gap tolerances, primal,
dual, and termination status, objective, best bound, reported gap, solve time,
node count where exposed, preprocessing audit, warm-start source, selected
original identifiers, and log location.

Every returned vector must already be binary under the declared exact
acceptance rule; no fractional candidate is rounded. Reconstruction is followed
by independent exact checks of mandatory retention, tagged coverage, burden,
the original frontier, and the original identity closure. A returned
\texttt{OPTIMAL} status is solver evidence plus an exact feasibility recheck,
not exhaustive enumeration, a Lean theorem, or a formal optimality proof.
When exact preprocessing empties the residual model, the record states that no
solver was invoked.

The authoritative benchmark audit is
\path{experiments/algorithmic_compression_v2/RESULT_AUDIT.md}; machine
certificates and hashes are below
\path{experiments/algorithmic_compression_v2/results/audit/certificates/} and
\path{experiments/algorithmic_compression_v2/results/ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.csv}.
The complete MIP logs, worker handshakes, stdout, stderr, instances, and raw
TOML records are retained under the committed v2 result tree. No second
open-source MIP solver was pinned, so medium and large cases receive exact
feasibility and burden rechecks plus HiGHS bound/status/log diagnostics rather
than a second-solver optimality claim.

For the two financial source instances and each of the three prospectively
locked weight schedules, fixed-point exact preprocessing leaves zero residual
strategies and zero residual requirements. The resulting six certificates
prove the optimum value by the human preprocessing propositions together with
exact finite computation and original-instance rechecks. They are not Lean
verified and do not use a solver status as proof. The authoritative rows are
\path{experiments/results/summaries/financial_resource_optimization_weight_robustness_empty_residual_certificates.csv}.

The audit command is nonmutating with respect to saved result records:
\begin{verbatim}
make aor-benchmark-v2-audit
\end{verbatim}
It reads the committed raw records and certificates; it does not rerun any
solver or heuristic.
