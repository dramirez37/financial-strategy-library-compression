# Audit

`ManuscriptLint.lean` is the release linter for the complete imported
development. It checks unused arguments across `StrategyInnovation` and
`simpNF` on the major theorem-bearing publication namespace families.
`AxiomAudit.lean` is the comprehensive release axiom audit: its 752 distinct
commands include all 276 distinct declarations and definitions named in the
active manuscript correspondence. The preprint report is
`../../../PREPRINT_LEAN_AUDIT.md`; the claim-by-claim correspondence is in
`../../../THEOREM_LEDGER.md`.

`Foundations.lean` records `#print axioms` output for the principal
finite-library lemmas. `DynamicInnovation.lean` does the same for the abstract
equivalence, quotient value, Markov sufficiency, and explicit minimality
family. `FrontierClosure.lean` audits modular factorization, identifiability,
the iff characterization, sufficiency, and exact counterexamples.
`FiniteHorizon.lean` audits the exact finite-state Bellman calculus. All run
focused environment linters. `Contraction.lean` audits the discounted
real-valued contraction, unique fixed point, convergence, geometric bound,
rational finite-horizon bridge, and dynamic-equivalence invariant.
`UnifiedCanonical.lean` audits all 36 theorem declarations in the exact
raw-derived six-state fixture, including its finite-sum reduction lemmas and
the twelve publication-facing benchmark certificates.
`Decomposition.lean` audits the total,
operational, and generative innovation decomposition and its monotonicity
boundary. `InnovationEquation.lean` audits the passive finite-horizon Strategy
Innovation Equation, reachability criterion, diminishing-marginal theorem, and
delayed-benefit example. The repository gate separately scans all Lean source
for prohibited placeholders and proof-critical markers.

`CoveragePotential.lean` audits the finite occupation representation.
`SingleGap.lean` audits the finite-order geometry definitions, monotone-gap
one-shot upper-threshold theorem, cutoff comparative statics, and exact
kernel/cost counterexamples.
`MultiGapRegion.lean` audits the exact two-gap disconnected cost-covering
construction and the arbitrary-cost boundary counterexample.

`RawModel.lean` audits the exact generation--verification admission law, raw
failure/success updates, closure absorption, module/frontier/closure insertion
updates, and the local raw-to-compressed identity. It does not audit or alter
the independent primitive transition and Bellman layers.

`RawToCompressed.lean` audits T1's derived normalized compressed law,
compression-fiber invariance, embedded controlled semi-Markov projection,
finite calendar-horizon value identity, discounted fixed-point identity, and
optimal compressed-policy lift. Its main theorems do not assume the optional
belief-path/admission conditional-independence predicate.

`UnifiedDynamicInnovation.lean` audits the publication-facing cost-sensitive
equivalence relation, raw finite- and infinite-value preservation, quotient
finiteness/factorization, compressed-state sufficiency, and the restricted
representation-refinement proposition. The older `DynamicInnovation.lean`
audit is retained only for the deprecated primitive supporting layer.

`RawFrontierClosure.lean` audits T2's raw generation/admission factorization
consequences, T1-projected transition equality, detectable converse, exact
frontier--closure/compressed-state criteria, and the raw-identifier and silent-
closure counterexamples. Candidate or admission changes count as detectable
only when they survive into the availability-tagged projected joint law.

`UnifiedSafeDeletion.lean` audits T3's raw operational/generative deletion
predicates, compressed-state identity, unified observation and finite/infinite
value preservation, stationary action comparisons, detectable converse,
rechecked deletion traces, pruning specification, and all exact
classification/order/recheck examples.

`NormalizedPruningLoss.lean` audits T4's parameter bounds, raw bridge
properties, survival/admission probability calculation, exact loss, sharp cap,
ratio, unit normalization, scaling corollary, and continued-operation formula.

`UnifiedDecomposition.lean` audits T5's raw/compressed passive and full values,
the insertion decomposition, silent-insertion consequences, operational
antitonicity, explicit closure-enrichment project dominance, premium
monotonicity, and the exact positive-generative bridge witness.

`GenerativeLowerBound.lean` audits T6's retained-carrier compatibility
surface. `JointDescendantLowerBound.lean` audits the dedicated generalized
joint-law interface: unit-interval mass, exact commitment accounting, the
primary lower bound and factorization corollary, mass/gain/cost orders, exact

`ComparativeStatics.lean` audits the sign-definite finite comparative-static

`DiscountSurvivalInteraction.lean` audits the exact finite matrix-power

`KernelComparativeStatics.lean` audits the parameterized two-state persistence

`SystemInteraction.lean` audits T7's closure increment and cross difference,
