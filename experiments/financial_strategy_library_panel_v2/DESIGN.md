# Registered Financial Innovation-Challenge Panel v2

**Protocol date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** design locked after all v1 outcomes, before any v2 outcome.

## Decision and claim boundary

The flagship question is no longer whether an oversized synthetic catalog can
be compressed. It is whether preserving the source library's declared
capability closure changes a prespecified next-year innovation decision after
holding burden fixed and preserving the same predecision operating frontier.

The primary comparison is paired within decision origin, source docket, burden
schedule, and masked innovation menu:

1. `innovation_safe_exact`, the minimum-burden library preserving all registered
   frontier and capability rows; and
2. `frontier_only_budget_matched`, the frontier-only optimum augmented in a
   frozen predecision order without exceeding the safe library's burden.

Both arms therefore preserve the same 30-row operating frontier and face the
same burden cap. They differ in whether closure is protected. The next-year
score is opened only after the universe, docket, menus, arms, selected
innovation candidate, and exact certificates are sealed.

This is a prospective v2 transformation and analysis, not a prospectively
collected dataset. Every v1 result is known. V1 and v2 are never pooled, and v1
rows do not enter a v2 estimand. The study remains a retrospective licensed-data
experiment and makes no causal, alpha, forecasting, deployable-performance,
population-savings, or observed-institutional-cost claim.

## Why this is a new study

The v1 lock is immutable. V1 lost eight of twenty origins because one sparse
security--belief cell invalidated the whole origin, and only three origins
supported its held-out belief profiles. Its full-factorial source contained a
median 14,400 active strategies against only 32 tagged requirements, making
near-total reduction largely a source-denominator fact. V2 changes the unit,
source construction, profile grid, arms, held-out decision, availability rule,
timing protocol, and primary estimand. Calling those changes an amendment would
erase the evidentiary chronology.

## Point-in-time chronology

The 20 origins remain 2005--2024. For origin year \(y\):

- years \(y-4,\ldots,y-2\) define belief cutpoints, profile support, source
  docket ranks, and challenge-candidate construction scores;
- years \(y-1,y\) define the 30-row compression frontier, burdens, and the
  frozen ranking used inside every masked challenge menu;
- year \(y+1\) supplies the postdecision utility of the already selected menu
  candidate.

The run order is enforced as a firewall: freeze and hash the origin universe;
freeze and hash both dockets; generate and hash every challenge menu; solve and
certify every arm; select and hash one candidate per arm and menu; only then
open postdecision returns.

## Origin eligibility fixes the v1 attrition mechanism

The v1 rule waited until candidate construction to discover that a selected ETF
lacked 25 observations in one belief state, then failed all nine instances at
that origin. V2 evaluates the same support condition as an origin-time security
eligibility rule. A failing security is excluded with a reason before candidate
generation. The origin fails only if fewer than 20 supported ETFs remain or the
SPY reference fails. Future rows may not affect this decision.

The universe retains at most the 100 most liquid supported ETFs. This cap both
reduces denominator-driven compression and ensures that every included
security can populate every predecision scenario. No postdecision completeness
condition may change the universe, source docket, arm, menu, selected candidate,
or burden.

## Robust frontier and compact research dockets

The source frontier crosses five SPY return states, three transaction-cost
levels (0, 5, and 15 basis points), and two risk-aversion values (1 and 3), for
30 exact frozen frontier rows. This replaces the v1 five-row frontier with a
decision-relevant robustness surface while preserving the identity-closure
algorithmic boundary.

Two compact source dockets replace the three stylized v1 organizations:

- `focused_docket`: the top two base-scenario strategies per eligible ETF, the
  top three per robust scenario, and at least three best carriers per
  capability;
- `broad_docket`: the top five per ETF, the top five per robust scenario, and at
  least five best carriers per capability.

Every exact cutoff tie is retained. These are prespecified research shortlists,
not observed institutional inventories. The full 96-row-per-ETF grammar is a
candidate pool, not an active-maintenance denominator.

## Burden-matched experimental arms

The six arms have distinct jobs:

- `source`: identifies the uncompressed reference.
- `innovation_safe_exact`: supplies the primary innovation-safe arm and exact
  burden benchmark.
- `frontier_only_exact`: measures the minimum burden price of protecting
  closure.
- `frontier_only_budget_matched`: isolates closure at the safe burden cap and
  is the primary comparator.
- `innovation_safe_greedy`: measures the quality of the certified greedy
  workflow.
- `innovation_safe_multistart_64`: measures the strongest registered deletion
  baseline.

The budget-matched arm starts at the frontier-only optimum and scans every
remaining strategy in descending pooled compression-window score, breaking
exact ties by stable identifier. It adds a whole strategy whenever that
strategy fits within the safe exact burden and otherwise continues to the next
strategy. It never uses a menu, a postdecision return, or observed closure
benefit.

Because burdens are indivisible, this arm is matched to the same registered
budget, not guaranteed to spend exactly the same burden. Realized burden and
unused slack are both reported for every paired cell.

## Masked innovation challenges

Each origin and docket receives 32 seeded challenge menus of eight candidates.
Candidates come from the origin-valid grammar but are excluded from the frozen
source docket. Menus are stratified by signal, signal--filter interface, and
risk rule, and are hashed before any arm is solved.

Within a menu, an arm chooses the highest predecision pooled score among
candidates whose registered capability requirements are carried by its retained
library. If none is generatable, it chooses the mandatory inactive cash option.
The selected identity is frozen before postdecision data are opened. The
primary outcome is the next-year full-calendar mean--variance score of that
choice under 5 basis points and risk aversion 3.

This makes availability partly mechanical but performance nonmechanical. Safe
closure guarantees access to every menu candidate whose requirements lie in the
source closure; it does not guarantee that the predecision choice performs well
next year. A frontier-only arm can choose another candidate or cash. The paired
score difference can therefore be positive, zero, or negative.

## Held-out completeness without post-outcome deletion

The primary postdecision score is pooled across the complete next-year trading
calendar and has no belief-state minimum. A valid delisting transition moves the
position to cash after the observed delisting return. An unobserved terminal
delisting return is never imputed.

If the already selected menu candidate has an unresolved return, that menu is
unavailable; the evaluator may not switch to the next candidate. An origin
needs 24 of 32 paired menus for the origin-level primary estimate. At least 15
origins and 75% of origins must pass this menu gate. If not, the held-out
flagship conclusion is registered as inconclusive; structural results remain
reportable and the thresholds cannot be relaxed.

## Population and computational blocks

All eligible population-block instances are retained, including trivial
preprocessing endpoints. Their frequency is descriptive for the registered
dockets.

A separately labeled computational stress block admits only instances with at
least three carriers per requirement and, after registered preprocessing, at
least 12 residual variables and 8 residual requirements. This block is designed
to compare methods on nontrivial exact-cover work and makes no population
prevalence claim. Compilation and warm-up occur outside timing. Five
steady-state repetitions record search, preprocessing, reconstruction, and
certification separately.

## Public evidence boundary

Raw CRSP rows and row-level return derivatives remain local. Public promotion
requires an independent whitelist audit and may include origin-level paired
contrasts, burdens, statuses, counts, hashes, exact certificates, and a fully
synthetic structural replay. The replay demonstrates the analysis and
certification path; it is not a substitute for the licensed financial result.

## Publication gate

V2 may replace v1 as the article's flagship financial experiment only after:

1. the design lock predates every v2 outcome;
2. the information-firewall and synthetic execution tests pass;
3. every registered origin, arm, menu, and failure has a terminal record;
4. independent structural, held-out, and public-boundary audits pass;
5. the registered availability gate passes; and
6. the manuscript reports the result even if the primary contrast is null or
   negative.
