\title[Financial strategy-library compression]{Financial Strategy Library Compression under Partial Information: Resource Constraints and Generative Value}

\author*[1]{\fnm{David} \sur{Ramirez}}\email{ramirezdavv@gmail.com}
\affil*[1]{\orgname{\AORAffiliationDisplay}}

\abstract{Growing financial strategy libraries consume validation, monitoring,
maintenance, computing, and governance resources. Retained strategies can be
operating assets, contributing current payoff, and generative assets, carrying
modules needed to produce future descendants. We study a finite
partial-information process whose raw library dynamics project onto a
sufficient productive state comprising the operating frontier and generative
closure. Exact, source-relative compression minimizes additive retention
burden while preserving both objects and therefore productive value and the
complete set of available optimal actions. Under identity closure, the exact
decision problem is NP-complete by a weight-preserving set-cover reduction;
the general optimization problem is NP-hard. Rechecking frontier and closure
after each deletion certifies a safe path, but a deletion-irreducible endpoint
need not be globally resource-minimal. A parameterized identity-closure family
shows that the specific heaviest-safe-first deletion rule has no constant
approximation guarantee, while other deletion orders can reach the optimum. A
sharp bridge construction shows that frontier-only pruning can eliminate the
complete attainable delayed descendant margin. Hard-capacity value is stepwise
and can exhibit increasing increments,
whereas penalized value is a convex piecewise-affine envelope with discrete
changes in the selected library. Exact rational fixtures, complete
small-library searches, registered synthetic stress tests, and retrospective
financial audits examine these mechanisms. Heuristic and mixed-integer outputs
remain numerical evidence: selected libraries are independently rechecked for
exact frontier equality, closure equality, burden, and mandatory inactive
retention. Held-out financial outcomes are used only after compression and
support no causal, forecasting, alpha, or deployable-performance claim.}

\keywords{strategy-library compression, partial information, stochastic control,
combinatorial optimization, generative value, quantitative finance}
