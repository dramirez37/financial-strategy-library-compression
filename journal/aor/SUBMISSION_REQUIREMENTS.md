## Manuscript requirements used by this wrapper

- LaTeX source and compiled PDF are supplied.
- The Springer Nature journal-article template is used with the author--year
  math/physical-sciences bibliography style.
- The abstract must contain 150--250 words.
- Four to six indexing keywords are supplied.
- The title page includes author, corresponding email, ORCID, and an
  affiliation or unaffiliated city/country once confirmed.
- A `Declarations` section before the reference list covers funding,
  competing interests, ethics/consent, data availability, code availability,
  and author contributions.
- Online Resource 1 is a standalone PDF with title, journal context, author,
  affiliation, and corresponding email once confirmed.

Official author instructions:
<https://link.springer.com/journal/10479/submission-guidelines>

## Repository-enforced scientific boundary

- The `v0.1.1-arxiv` release tree and PDFs remain unchanged.
- The frozen registered N=1024 study remains unchanged and is not replayed by
  the targeted journal gate.
- No raw or row-level CRSP/WRDS data enter the journal bundle.
- Complexity is a human-readable proof with exact Julia reduction validation;
  it is not described as Lean-verified.
- Exact enumeration, heuristic search, mixed-integer solver status, synthetic
  evidence, and retrospective financial evidence remain distinct.
- No causal, forecasting, alpha, or deployable-performance claim is made.
