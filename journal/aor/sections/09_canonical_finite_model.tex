\section{Controlled experiments}\label{sec:experiments}

The experiments separate four evidentiary roles. Exact rational fixtures
exercise individual optimization mechanisms; a bounded deterministic search
compares exact enumeration with heuristic candidates; a registered randomized
study summarizes behavior across finite generated libraries; and the two
financial audits ask retrospectively how much resource an exact
frontier--closure constraint costs in two larger libraries. The first three
layers are finite computational or synthetic evidence; the financial
information boundary is stated in Appendix~E. Full protocols and diagnostic
tables are in Online Supplements~S5--S7.

\subsection{Exact optimization fixtures}

The smallest exact fixtures are reported where their mechanisms are used:
the local--global compression witness in Section~4, the sharp bridge loss in
Section~4.5, and the capacity and price geometry in Section~6.  Online
Supplement~S4 gives the complete canonical resource correspondences, and
Online Supplement~S5 retains the broader mechanism suite.  All fixture values
use exact rational arithmetic and complete finite enumeration.

Capacity rises quickly but not concavely: on average, three quarters of source
burden recovers more than $99\%$ of attainable productive value, yet many
attainable staircases contain an increasing increment.
Frontier-only compression is more aggressive precisely because it may discard
the descendant channel; its zero median normalized loss coexists with a sharp
upper tail.
