# Financial Strategy-Library Panel v1 — reporting rules

1. Always use the full study title and the versioned experiment identifier.
2. State that universes are reconstructed as of each origin from a current
   CRSP snapshot; never call them vintage-database certified.
3. Report all 20 origin records, including universe failures.
4. Keep the three library constructions separate before any explicitly labeled
   cross-construction summary.
5. Keep burden schedules in their registered units; never translate work or
   review units into money or labor hours.
6. Distinguish human proof, Lean verification, exact finite computation,
   floating-point profile construction, MIP solver evidence, synthetic evidence,
   and retrospective licensed-data evidence.
7. A solver `OPTIMAL` status is not formal or exhaustive proof. Report the
   termination status, incumbent, best bound, gap, and exact incumbent checks.
8. Never round a fractional binary vector into a retained library.
9. Preserve failures, timeouts, inapplicability, missing diagnostics, and
   certificate rejections in all denominators.
10. Report exact ties completely at source-construction cutoffs. Algorithmic
    optimizer identities need not be complete unless a method explicitly
    enumerates every tie.
11. Describe capability ownership as active-maintenance responsibility in the
    registered catalog, not intellectual property, sole storage, or real-world
    institutional indispensability.
12. Report postdecision diagnostics only after structural results and identify
    their maximum-over-catalog and selection limitations.
13. Make no causal, forecasting, alpha, population-savings, or deployable-
    performance claim.
14. Do not pool the historical terminal and annual audits with this panel.
15. Do not pool benchmark v2 runtime with this study's runtime.
16. Public outputs may contain aggregate strategy specifications, capability
    identifiers, hashes, burdens, statuses, and certificates. They may not
    contain raw or row-level licensed observations or vendor metadata rows.
17. Public promotion is separate, explicit, and allowed only after the licensed
    run and independent exact/public-boundary audits pass.
18. Every amendment preserves this lock, is numbered and dated, enumerates
    outcomes already accessible, and states whether it changes science,
    implementation only, or reporting only.
