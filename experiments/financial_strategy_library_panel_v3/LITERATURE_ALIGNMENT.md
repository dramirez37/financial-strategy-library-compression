# Literature alignment and design commitments

This file records which safeguards are part of the protocol and which are only
diagnostics. It does not promote the known 2000--2025 history to confirmatory
evidence.

## Search and selection

The complete proposal candidate family is the object of uncertainty analysis.
Every candidate and failure enters a frozen trial ledger. The primary policies
use simultaneous moving-block max-t lower bounds rather than pointwise winner
tests. A full-family White Reality Check and Hansen SPA analysis are registered
diagnostics for any strategy-level performance discussion; neither substitutes
for the held-out policy contrast.

This implements the central warning in [White (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-0262.00152)
that the best model found in a specification search must be assessed against
the search itself. [Hansen (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1198/073500105000000063)
provides the complementary superior-predictive-ability diagnostic.

Deflated Sharpe and probability-of-backtest-overfitting summaries are secondary
search diagnostics. They may describe the candidate family but may not become
the primary estimand or repair a failed held-out policy result.

## Point-in-time membership and delistings

Primary eligibility is fixed using predecision data. Future survival is never
required. Observed delisting returns are retained and unresolved terminal
returns remain explicit failures. This is the direct design response to the
missing-terminal-return bias documented by
[Shumway (1997)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1997.tb03818.x).

## Trading costs and capacity

The primary historical contrast retains a transparent fixed one-way cost for
comparability. A registered capacity surface adds lagged spread and square-root
impact at fixed AUM and participation levels. These results are sensitivity
evidence rather than a claim of executable institutional profit. The design is
motivated by the proportional and non-proportional trading-cost treatment in
[Korajczyk and Sadka (2004)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00656.x).

## Dependence and finite-panel reporting

The compression-decision origin is the indivisible financial unit. Securities
and candidate strategies are not replications. Adjacent origins share calendar
history, so origin-level intervals and leave-one-origin-out summaries are
descriptive. The paper must lead with the complete finite panel, effect sizes,
signs, and influence rather than asymptotic significance.

## Operations-research contribution

The principal AOR evidence is the exact, independently certified comparison of
minimum-burden libraries under frozen frontier and closure constraints. The
economic block tests a decision policy induced by those libraries. It is not a
stand-alone trading-strategy discovery exercise.
