# Calibrated Closure-Option Mechanism Experiment

## Journal disposition

This is the article's synthetic mechanism experiment. It belongs in the
economic-implications section and is paired with, but not substituted for, the
retrospective financial study.

The innovation-safe and frontier-only libraries have equal current frontiers
and equal burdens. Only the safe library retains the bridge capability for one
declared research project. Aggregate market data calibrate proposal-noise
scale, serial dependence, and tail thickness; they do not choose a regime's
sign. The powered margin is fixed at twice an independently calibrated null
threshold before test-world simulation.

## Audited result

Each prespecified regime contains 4,096 worlds. The complete 16,384-row ledger
was independently regenerated, record hashes were verified, and the registered
summary was reproduced byte for byte.

| Regime | Learned mean [95% interval] | Adoption |
|---|---:|---:|
| Adverse margin | -0.000011 [-0.000027, 0.000004] | 0.05% |
| Null margin | -0.000152 [-0.000318, 0.000015] | 5.76% |
| Low positive dose | 0.000051 [-0.000137, 0.000239] | 7.35% |
| Powered positive dose | 0.051227 [0.050002, 0.052452] | 94.17% |

The null point adoption rate satisfies the registered 6.5% finite-test gate,
but its Wilson upper endpoint is 6.52%; both facts must remain visible. The
low-dose oracle value is positive while the learned interval crosses zero. The
powered result is recovery at a prospectively chosen power-design dose, not an
estimated market return premium.

## Evidence and claim boundary

The structural fixture checks are exact finite computations. The value,
adoption, and uncertainty results are synthetic floating-point evidence. They
are not Lean verification, a historical securities-panel result, a causal
estimate, alpha evidence, or deployable-performance evidence.
