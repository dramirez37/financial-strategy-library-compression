# V4 claim boundary

V4 may support this claim:

> In a market-calibrated, closure-active mechanism environment satisfying the
> registered AOR bridge condition, innovation-safe compression preserves a
> strictly positive research-option value that an equal-burden frontier-only
> library cannot access.

It may additionally report whether the registered learner recovers that value
and whether its adoption frequency is controlled under null and adverse
conditions.

V4 may not be described as evidence of historical or future market alpha,
deployable profitability, a causal return premium, or confirmation in an
untouched securities panel. V3 remains the empirical external-validity result
and remains a transparent null for its primary common-equity policy contrast.
