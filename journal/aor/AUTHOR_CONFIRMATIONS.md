# Author confirmations required before submission

The repository does not establish the personal or disclosure facts below.
They remain explicit blockers rather than inferred statements. Replace the
matching fields in `author_metadata.tex` only after the author confirms:

1. institutional affiliation, or city and country if unaffiliated;
2. funding/support statement;
3. relevant financial and non-financial competing interests; and
4. the sole-author contribution statement.

`make journal-check` permits the visible placeholders for development.
`make journal-submission-check` and `build_submission_bundle.sh` reject them.

Before upload, the author must also confirm that the manuscript is not under
consideration elsewhere and approve the final preprint-disclosure wording.
