# Proposal-Known Robustness Freeze Amendment 004

## Information state

The five primary proposal choices and proposal-year returns are known.  No
evaluation-year return has been inspected, materialized, or used.  This amendment
therefore cannot alter the primary specification or promote a favorable sensitivity
to primary status.  It freezes registered proposal-stage robustness choices before
evaluation access, so evaluation can later score them without reselection.

## Executable proposal grid

For each gate-passed cell, the comparator and innovation-safe policies are frozen for
the complete Cartesian grid of one-way costs `0, 5, 15, 30` basis points, risk
aversions `1, 3`, and annual adoption hurdles `0, 0.0025, 0.005`.  The candidate
action sets and proposal sessions are exactly those sealed before proposal access.  A
path must be complete on every proposal session to enter inference.

Each cost-risk pair uses 5,000 paired circular moving-block resamples of 20 sessions
and the stable registered proposal-max-t seed.  The comparator is simultaneously
tested against cash.  The safe critical value is computed jointly over every
complete safe candidate crossed with every complete comparator candidate plus cash,
before the data-dependent comparator is selected.  Family shrinkage and the frozen
identifier tie-break are unchanged.  Hurdles alter adoption only, so the same sealed
cost-risk inference is reused across its three hurdle decisions.

## Registered design dispositions

For common equities, the top-50 membership is reconstructible from the preproposal
sealed liquidity ranks inside the frozen top-100 panel and receives a primary-rule
choice freeze.  Top 100 is the primary membership and is bound directly to the
primary grid choice.  Top 200 cannot be reconstructed because securities ranked
101--200 and their histories were not staged before proposal access; it is retained
as unavailable with `DESIGN_NOT_FROZEN_BEFORE_PROPOSAL`.  Common-equity cap records
are not applicable to ETF cells.

The registered permuted-closure sham seed exists, but no sham library, capability
permutation, or sham action-set mapping was frozen before proposal values were known.
It is retained for every cell as unavailable with
`DESIGN_NOT_FROZEN_BEFORE_PROPOSAL`; no post-outcome sham mechanics are invented.

Leave-one-origin-out is aggregation-only and may not select a new strategy.  Capacity
will score already-frozen choices only, with no reselection, after a separate
pre-evaluation lock freezes the AUM grid, spread/impact field mapping, impact formula,
and participation rule.  Until that lock exists, capacity results remain deferred.

Public artifacts contain only design parameters, counts, availability, and hashes.
Exact strategy identities and proposal-derived inference details remain in sealed,
ignored local artifacts.  Evaluation access remains prohibited until this robustness
result is sealed and replay-audited.
