# Financial panel v2 design amendment 002 — outcome-blind flagship restart

The completed 2000–2025 master panel showed that the revised liquid equity-and-ETF universe is ample: every origin has at least 1,179 securities with a complete usable predecision reference calendar. The interrupted predecision pilot nevertheless classified only 12 of 20 origins as structurally evaluable. The failure was caused by the remaining legacy rule requiring 25 observations in every SPY state in both formation windows. Because the state is common to all securities, a rare market regime removed every security at once. This reproduced the central design failure the revision was meant to eliminate.

This amendment removes state-cell support from security eligibility. Eligibility now depends only on the frozen point-in-time classification, prespecified liquidity rules, and a complete usable predecision calendar. The five regime counts remain visible diagnostics. Each of the 30 regime/cost/risk coordinates is estimated using a locked linear shrinkage rule: `n/(n+25)` times the state-specific compression utility plus `25/(n+25)` times pooled compression utility. An empty state therefore uses the pooled value. The inherited value 25 is a prior-strength parameter, not an exclusion threshold.

The amendment was informed by the predecision universe census, candidate scores, consumption of the original registered seeds, and the start of arm solving. It was not informed by a serialized predecision result, a predecision seal, a postdecision extraction, or any held-out outcome. The interrupted pilot is excluded from all flagship estimands. The restart uses a new immutable seed namespace and a separate registered seed file; no predecessor seed may be reused.

The 38,327,975-row master panel built under execution lock 008 is adopted byte-for-byte. Its complete directory aggregate and manifest digest are bound by the successor locks and revalidated before use. Licensed rows remain local and are never public artifacts.
