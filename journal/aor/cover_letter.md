Dear Professor Giacometti and Guest Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Financial Strategy Library Compression under
Partial Information: Resource Constraints and Generative Value” as an
Original Research submission to the special issue *Advances in Quantitative
Finance and Risk Modeling*.

The paper studies a quantitative-finance operations problem that appears when
a growing strategy library consumes validation, monitoring, computing, and
governance resources while retained strategies also carry modules needed to
generate later descendants. Its central contribution is an exact separation
between the operating frontier and generative closure. The paper proves when
those objects form a sufficient productive state, formulates minimum-burden
innovation-safe compression, establishes the finite capacity and
resource-price geometry, and gives a human proof that the exact decision
problem is NP-complete under identity closure. The complexity theorem is not
presented as Lean-verified.

The evidence is deliberately layered. Human proofs and selected Lean kernel
checks are distinguished from exact finite computation, floating-point
evidence, mixed-integer solver evidence, registered synthetic evidence, and
retrospective financial evidence. Every solver-returned library reported in
the financial audits is independently rechecked for exact frontier equality,
exact closure equality, exact burden, and retention of the mandatory inactive
strategy. The empirical exercises use held-out outcomes only after compression
and make no causal, forecasting, alpha, or deployable-performance claim.

An earlier `v0.1.1-arxiv` preprint release candidate is preserved in the public
repository. The submitted journal version adds the explicit complexity result,
integrates the bounded algorithmic evidence, and supplies journal-specific
declarations without changing the meanings of the preprint's theorems or the
frozen registered studies.
