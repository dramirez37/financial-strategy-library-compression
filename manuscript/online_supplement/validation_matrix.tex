\subsection{Status categories}

\begingroup
\footnotesize
The evidence columns are independent.  \emph{Human-readable proof} means a
mathematical derivation in the manuscript or proof appendices.  \emph{Formal
verification} means an exact machine-checked counterpart that builds under
the recorded assumptions and axiom audit.  \emph{Exact rational validation}
means selected finite calculations performed without rounding.
\emph{Floating-point validation} means numerical output accompanied by stated
residual, error, tolerance, or drift gates.  \emph{Synthetic experiment}
means a controlled fixture, response surface, or registered randomized
finite-library design.  \emph{Retrospective financial audit} means one of the two
locked retrospective audits; it is descriptive rather than causal.

The matrix uses a controlled status vocabulary.  \textbf{Complete} means the
channel covers the displayed result under its stated assumptions.
\textbf{Supporting} means it covers a consequence, implementation, or special
case but not the whole result.  \textbf{Stress-tested} means the mechanism was
exercised across a registered synthetic design.  \textbf{Illustrated} means
the mechanism appears in a locked financial audit.  \textbf{Adverse} marks a
prespecified financial estimand with the opposite sign to the proposed
ranking; \textbf{Mixed} marks heterogeneous or noisy signs.
\textbf{Not assessed} means the channel is relevant but has no registered
result, while \textbf{N/A} means the channel is not applicable.
\endgroup

Joint descendant-event generative-option lower bound &
\Cref{sec:value} &
