# SSRN requirements verified September 18, 2026

The workflow prepares the files; it does not submit through an SSRN account.

| Official source | Requirement and implementation |
|---|---|
| [Submission guidelines](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/get-started), August 3, 2026 | English title, abstract and full-text PDF; revision date; author names, current affiliations and valid emails; account/profile and applicable copyright permission. Identity and affiliation appear in PDFs and metadata. AI disclosure is in the PDF and submission abstract. Profile and portal attestations remain upload-time actions. |
| [Generative-AI policy](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/AI), August 3, 2026 | Disclose material AI use, tools and scope; human authors retain responsibility. The existing ChatGPT/Codex disclosure is retained, with no AI authorship. |
| [PDF troubleshooting](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/pdf-issues), August 3, 2026 | Genuine PDF, embedded fonts, selectable text, no passwords or printing/copying restrictions. Automated checks validate these properties. |
| [Abstract field](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/what-is-needed-in-the-abstract-section-of-the-submission-form), August 3, 2026 | Paste the abstract with paragraph separation. `abstract_for_ssrn.txt` includes the disclosure paragraph. |
| [Research integrity](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/research-integrity), February 25, 2026 | SSRN screens integrity and editorial suitability; it does not peer review preprints. Assumptions, evidence classes, negative results and data access are explicit. |
| [Submission-form guide](https://supportcontent.elsevier.com/Support%20Hub/SSRN/NewSubmissionForm_update3Oct2024.pdf), October 2024 | Guide states a 100 MB upload limit. The package check conservatively uses 100,000,000 bytes; confirm the live limit on upload day. |

Additional project quality controls include metadata consistency, complete
supporting proofs, bounded empirical claims, preserved null/adverse findings,
source and PDF hashes, source completeness, link checks and visual review.
No universal word/page limit or exact live eJournal name is assumed.
