# Result Summaries

Compact reproducible summaries in Markdown, TOML, JSON, or CSV may be tracked
here.

- `compression_experiments.json` is the structured summary for
  `innovation-safe-library-compression-v1`.
- `compression_experiments.csv` contains the same eight result rows for direct
  analysis. Exact rational fields use canonical `numerator//denominator`
  strings; unavailable checks are JSON `null` and empty CSV cells.

Both files are generated by
`julia/scripts/run_compression_experiments.jl`. Runtime fields are
host-dependent and are not expected to be byte-identical across machines.

- `unified_canonical_summary.json` records the raw catalog/library and
  compressed-state counts, exact residuals, Float64 a-posteriori contraction
  bound, exactly re-evaluated residual-based certificate, and every
  raw/compressed equality-check count.
- `unified_canonical_values.csv` and `unified_canonical_policies.csv` record
  horizon-zero through horizon-eight exact solutions plus stationary exact
  policy iteration, exact/Float64 policy evaluation, and Float64 value
  iteration.
- `unified_canonical_transition_edges.csv`,
  `unified_canonical_duration_paths.csv`, and
  `unified_canonical_operating_rewards.csv` expose the derived compressed
  transitions, every positive joint path/admission atom, and every unified
  operating-reward block.
- `unified_canonical_convergence.csv` contains all 42 Float64 iterations with
  explicitly labeled Float64 increments, residuals, and contraction bounds;
  the exactly re-evaluated certificate is recorded separately in
  `unified_canonical_summary.json`.  The
  `unified_canonical_comparative_statics.csv` contains the exact cost,
  duration, discount, admission, and survival fixtures.

These files are generated from raw primitives by
`julia/scripts/solve_unified_canonical_benchmark.jl`; exact rational fields
use canonical `numerator//denominator` strings.

- `unified_canonical_resource_summary.json` records the preregistered weight
  schedules, output counts, and exact certificate aggregates.
- `unified_canonical_resource_libraries.csv` reports burden, productive/net,
  operational, generative, duration, action, frontier, and closure data for
  every schedule, belief, and raw library.
- `unified_canonical_resource_safe_compression.csv`,
  `unified_canonical_resource_capacity.csv`, and
  `unified_canonical_resource_penalized_intervals.csv` retain the complete
  exact optimizer correspondences and exhaustive certificates.
- `unified_canonical_resource_switching_prices.csv` distinguishes all
  pairwise intersections from globally active envelope switches, while
  `unified_canonical_resource_channel_elasticities.csv` gives the exact
  discount derivative, duration, channel elasticity, and contribution table.

These files are generated deterministically by
`julia/scripts/run_unified_resource_benchmark.jl`. Exact CSVs are the source
of truth; the associated SVGs use `Float64` coordinates only for rendering.

The historical `canonical_model_{summary,convergence,policy}` artifacts remain
unchanged. They are generated by `solve_canonical_model.jl` and are retained
only as primitive-timing regression and appendix compatibility fixtures.

- `coverage_geometry_summary.json` records the exact S4/S5/C2 fixture checks,
  baseline parameters, component/threshold classification, transverse boundary
  diagnostics, and the complete output inventory.
- `coverage_frontier_gap.csv`, `coverage_occupation_distribution.csv`,
  `coverage_potential.csv`, `coverage_research_boundaries.csv`, and
  `coverage_connected_disconnected.csv` are the source data for the five SVG
  figures.
- `coverage_sensitivity.csv` records every belief row for the baseline and 18
  one-at-a-time cost, discount, persistence, delay, survival, and signal-
  precision scenarios.

These seven files are generated deterministically by
`julia/scripts/run_coverage_geometry.jl`. Exact theorem fixtures remain
canonical rational strings in JSON; the larger finite geometry is Float64
numerical evidence only.

- `theorem_mechanism_summary.{json,csv}` records the pass/fail outcome and
  observed pattern for families A--G.
- `theorem_mechanism_policy_summary.csv` records all twelve dynamic-policy
  scenarios, cutoffs, iterations, residuals, and posterior error bounds.
- `theorem_mechanism_decomposition.csv` records the exact operational,
  generative, and total components.
- `theorem_mechanism_coverage_ranking.csv` records all six prospective
  candidates, exact gap vectors, target and comparator scores, ranks, and both
  configured perturbation regimes.
- `theorem_mechanism_coverage_ranking_summary.csv` and
  `theorem_mechanism_coverage_selection.csv` record the exact rank correlations,
  top-one regrets, and individual-versus-marginal top-two selection path.
- `theorem_mechanism_{pruning_loss,decomposition_figure,coverage_geometry,policy_map}.csv`
  plus the coverage-ranking tables are the complete sources for the five
  publication SVGs.
- `theorem_mechanism_metadata.json` records the Julia version, seed/RNG,
  arithmetic modes, command, source/config/manifest hashes, raw row count, and
  SHA-256 of every generated artifact.

All are generated by `julia/scripts/run_theorem_mechanism_experiments.jl`.
Exact families use `Rational{BigInt}`; the 61-belief policy map is seeded
Float64 numerical evidence retained for compatibility. The active Section 7
policy view is instead generated from
`unified_comparative_statics_surface.csv`, whose rows enforce the unified raw
source, strictly positive duration, and numerical solver gates.

- `financial_terminal_audit_strategy_grammar.csv` enumerates all 2,400 finite
  strategies over 25 ETFs.
- `financial_terminal_audit_candidate_audit.csv` records membership, enablement,
  gross/net utilities, and turnover for every strategy.
- `financial_terminal_audit_pruning_audit.csv` records every safe and
  frontier-only deletion decision.
- `financial_compression_policy_characteristics.csv` is the aggregate-only,
  publication-facing policy audit for both the locked terminal and annual
  walk-forward exercises. It records operational/generative deletion
  contributions, module uniqueness, dependent-candidate counts, and
  best-descendant support; every row is marked as numerical rather than
  theorem evidence.
- `financial_compression_predictor_summary.csv` groups those policy
  characteristics and explicitly flags the unidentifiable within-audit module
  uniqueness contrast.
- `financial_compression_estimand_metadata.csv` names \(Q_a(L')\) as ex post
  enabled-descendant opportunity quality and records that it is evaluated from
  held-out audit outcomes after pruning decisions are fixed. It marks
  `algorithm_input=false`, `forecast=false`, `policy_score=false`,
  `deployable_selection_criterion=false`, and separately records the
  innovation-safe and frontier-only acceptance tests.
- `../../../EMPIRICAL_INFORMATION_SET_AUDIT.md` supplies the complete
  quantity-by-quantity timing classification: pruning-time,
  validation/pre-target, held-out, retrospective descriptive, or oracle. It
  also distinguishes information that was structurally available from
  information actually used by pruning.
- `../../../manuscript/figures/financial_innovation_safe_compression.tex` is
  the Julia-generated three-panel safety--compression audit. At natural size
  it reports exact strategy and module retention fractions and separately
  scaled audit-specific ex post opportunity-quality cards. The bridge figure
  now owns the carrier-path mechanism, avoiding visual duplication. The
  financial figure does not pool incomparable units or reduce its declared
  `\scriptsize` body font through an enclosing resize.
- The ranking, decomposition, mechanism, uncertainty, and cost CSVs retain
  frozen comparisons, negative results, StableRNG block intervals, and cost
  sensitivities.
- The three `financial_terminal_audit_*_figure.csv` tables source the three
  script-generated SVGs; the status JSON records source, license, hashes, seed,
  decision hash, and publication gate.

These outputs are generated by `julia/scripts/run_financial_terminal_audit.jl`.
They are publishable mechanism evidence, not market-alpha estimates. The JSON
metadata records the separate nonredistribution rule for raw and row-level
licensed inputs. Legacy columns named `locked_candidate_quality_change` or
`future_candidate_quality_change` remain unchanged for locked-artifact lineage;
their reader-facing interpretation is governed by
`financial_compression_estimand_metadata.csv`.
