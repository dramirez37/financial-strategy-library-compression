# Optimization Problem Specification for Paper 1

## Status and non-formalization boundary

This document freezes the Paper 1 optimization domains under D-0112. It uses
the fixed additive rational burden in `RESOURCE_MODEL_SPEC.md` and leaves the
productive raw transition and value recursion unchanged.

The generic burden, capacity, and penalty definitions and the identity-closure
specialization of safe compression are implemented by the exact finite Julia
outer optimizer and exercised by the minimized counterexample audit. General
closure remains the primary mathematical definition. These computations
create exact certificates, not Lean theorem status or an experiment. Later
formalization must use these domains and the revised statements in
`OPTIMIZATION_THEOREM_REVISIONS.md`.

## 1. Common finite domain

Let \(S\) be the fixed finite master strategy catalog, and let
\[
  S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}\subseteq S
\]
be the strategies that are independently catalog-certified and eligible for
retention at the outer optimization date under parameter bundle \(\theta\).
Require
\[
  s_0\in S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}.
\]
Here “eligible” means independently catalog-certified for the outer retention
decision. It is not the stochastic raw verification event governed by
\(\nu\): in the existing raw process, a candidate that passes that event is
already represented by an admitted `some(s)` outcome.

The master catalog may also contain identifiers that can be generated by the
raw innovation process but are not outer-certified for retention. Those
identifiers are not selectable by an outer optimization problem.

In the existing raw terminology, a “generated but not admitted” candidate has
not appeared as an admitted `some(s)` outcome. Mere raw generation does not
make it selectable. It could enter an outer problem only after a separate
eligibility rule placed its identifier in \(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}\); the
primary specification defines no such raw-to-outer conversion. This is
different from an already outer-certified catalog strategy that simply is not
in the current library: the latter may be selected by a full-catalog capacity
or penalty problem.

For any finite \(A\subseteq S\) containing \(s_0\), define the admissible
library family
\[
  \mathfrak L(A):=\{L'\subseteq A:s_0\in L'\}.
\]
Every optimization variable called a library belongs to such a family.
Accordingly, the displayed shorthand \(L'\subseteq S\) in the capacity and
penalty problems means
\[
  L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}).
\]
If every master-catalog row is already outer-certified and selectable, then
\(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}=S\) and the shorthand is literal.

The parameter bundle \(\theta\) fixes the productive finite raw model,
including the catalog payoff and module tables, closure operator, generation
and verification inputs, project data, belief process, discount, and the
finite calendar horizon and terminal-payoff convention. The primary exact
optimization layer uses the resulting rational finite-horizon value
\[
  V_\theta(b,L')\in\mathbb Q.
\]
Resource weights are fixed catalog primitives as in
`RESOURCE_MODEL_SPEC.md`; \(B,\lambda\in\mathbb Q_{\ge0}\). A stationary-value
variant may be defined later by replacing \(V_\theta\) with the already
well-defined stationary productive value, but it is a separately labeled
variant and need not have a rational value representation.

All optimizer outputs are set-valued correspondences unless uniqueness is
proved separately. No deterministic tie breaker is part of these definitions.

## 2. Exact safe compression

Let \(L\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})\) be the current eligible
library. Define its exact safe-feasible sublibraries by
\[
  \mathfrak F_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)
  :=
  \left\{
    L'\in\mathfrak L(L):
    F_{L'}=F_L,\ C_{L'}=C_L
  \right\}.
\]
The equalities are exact: frontier equality is extensional equality at every
belief, and closure equality is equality of finite module sets.

The exact safe-compression problem is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  P_{\mathrm{safe}}(L):\qquad
  &\underset{L'}{\operatorname{minimize}}
  &&W(L')\\
  &\text{over}
  &&L'\in\mathfrak L(L)\\
  &\text{subject to}
  &&F_{L'}=F_L,\\
  &&&C_{L'}=C_L.
\end{aligned}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
  W_{\mathrm{safe}}^\star(L)
  :=
  \min_{L'\in\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)}W(L'),
\]
with optimizer correspondence
\[
  \operatorname{Opt}_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)
  :=
  \operatorname*{argmin}_{L'\in\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)}W(L').
\]

This problem is current-library compression: it may retain or delete
strategies already in \(L\), but it may not import a strategy from
\(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}\setminus L\). Exact safety is a feasibility
condition. Global minimum resource is the separate conclusion obtained only
after minimizing over the complete safe-feasible family.

## 3. Dynamic-equivalence form

Using the current unified cost-sensitive dynamic innovation relation, define
\[
  \mathfrak F_{\mathrm{DI}}(L)
  :=
  \left\{
    L'\in\mathfrak L(L):
    L'\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}L
  \right\}.
\]
The dynamic-equivalence formulation is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  &\underset{L'}{\operatorname{minimize}}
  &&W(L')\\
  &\text{over}
  &&L'\in\mathfrak L(L)\\
  &\text{subject to}
  &&L'\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}L.
\end{aligned}
\]

### Exact equivalence of the two formulations

The frontier--closure and dynamic-equivalence formulations have identical
feasible sets, objectives, optimal values, and optimizer correspondences
exactly when
\[
  \forall L'\in\mathfrak L(L),\qquad
  L'\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}L
  \iff
  \bigl(F_{L'}=F_L\ \land\ C_{L'}=C_L\bigr).
\]

In the current raw model, this biconditional is available under all of the
following conditions:

1. \(L\) and \(L'\) are admissible realizable libraries in the same fixed raw
   process and catalog;
2. generation, primitive admission, availability, cost, duration, operation,
   and the joint completion coupling obey the T1/UDI restrictions that make
   frontier--closure equality sufficient for identical productive
   observations; and
3. A-T2-OBS, implemented as `RawClosureDetectable`, holds on the relevant
   realizable frontier--closure pairs, so productive dynamic equivalence
   identifies closure as well as frontier.

The sufficiency direction
\[
  F_{L'}=F_L,\ C_{L'}=C_L
  \Longrightarrow
  L'\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}L
\]
uses the current raw factorization and does not require detectability. The
converse requires A-T2-OBS. Without detectability,
\[
  \mathfrak F_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)
  \subseteq
  \mathfrak F_{\mathrm{DI}}(L)
\]
may be strict because a closure difference can be behaviorally invisible.
The dynamic-equivalence problem may then attain a strictly lower burden than
exact frontier--closure compression, and the formulations must not be called
equivalent.

The relation \(\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}\) compares productive process observations;
it does not compare \(W\). That is correct here because \(W\) is the objective,
not part of the safety constraint.

## 4. Capacity-constrained retention

Capacity-constrained retention selects from the entire outer-certified
retention-eligible catalog, not merely from a current library:
\[
  V^\star(B;b,\theta)
  :=
  \max
  \left\{
    V_\theta(b,L'):
    L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}),\
    W(L')\le B
  \right\}.
\]
Under the full-eligibility shorthand this is
\[
  V^\star(B;b,\theta)
  =
  \max
  \left\{
    V_\theta(b,L'):
    L'\subseteq S,\ W(L')\le B
  \right\},
\]
where admissibility still requires \(s_0\in L'\).

The inactive-only library has burden zero, so this maximum is feasible and
attained for every \(B\in\mathbb Q_{\ge0}\). The canonical real-capacity
extension uses the same finite rational library pairs and the same inequality
for \(B\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}\); it is introduced only to state the
right-continuous step structure and one-sided breakpoint convention.

Define the optimizer correspondence
\[
  \operatorname{Opt}_{B}(b,\theta)
  :=
  \operatorname*{argmax}_{
    L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}),\ W(L')\le B
  }
  V_\theta(b,L').
\]

With the finite attainable-burden set
\[
  \Omega
  =
  \{W(L'):L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})\},
\]
an interior capacity-value breakpoint is a positive \(B\) satisfying
\[
  V^\star(B)>V^\star(B^-).
\]
Value breakpoints are distinct from thresholds where a newly feasible
library merely joins the tied optimizer correspondence. The complete theorem
and shape boundaries are in `CAPACITY_VALUE_SPEC.md`.

No frontier or closure preservation constraint is implicit. A
capacity-optimal library may sacrifice productive value relative to an
unconstrained library because insufficient capacity makes exact safety
infeasible. A current-library-restricted capacity problem may instead replace
\(\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})\) by \(\mathfrak L(L)\), but that is a
named restriction and is not the default definition of \(V^\star\).

## 5. Penalized retention

Penalized retention also selects from the entire outer-certified
retention-eligible catalog:
\[
  J^\star(\lambda;b,\theta)
  :=
  \max
  \left\{
    V_\theta(b,L')-\lambda W(L'):
    L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})
  \right\}.
\]
Under the full-eligibility shorthand this is
\[
  J^\star(\lambda;b,\theta)
  =
  \max
  \left\{
    V_\theta(b,L')-\lambda W(L'):
    L'\subseteq S
  \right\},
\]
again with \(s_0\in L'\).

The optimizer correspondence is
\[
  \operatorname{Opt}_{\lambda}(b,\theta)
  :=
  \operatorname*{argmax}_{L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})}
  \left\{
    V_\theta(b,L')-\lambda W(L')
  \right\}.
\]

The primary exact evaluator takes
\(\lambda\in\mathbb Q_{\ge0}\). For continuity, derivatives, and one-sided
slopes, `PENALIZED_ENVELOPE_SPEC.md` embeds the exact rational branch data in
the canonical extension \(\lambda\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}\). The two definitions
agree at every rational price. The feasible family remains fixed as price
changes.

There is no hard capacity and no exact-safety constraint in this definition.
Either may be added only in a separately named hybrid problem. The symbol
\(V_\theta\) remains productive value, while
\(V_\theta-\lambda W\) is net retained-library value.

## 6. Replacement and admission review

Let \(L\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})\) be the current library and
let
\[
  c\in S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}}\setminus L,
  \qquad
  c\ne s_0,
\]
be a newly outer-certified candidate presented at a retention review.
Outer certification is distinct from raw stochastic verification. The
candidate is conditionally required to enter the post-review library. The
planner chooses active strategies to delete:
\[
  D\subseteq L\setminus\{s_0\}.
\]
Define
\[
  L^{c,D}:=(L\setminus D)\cup\{c\}.
\]
The replacement problem is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  &\underset{D}{\operatorname{maximize}}
  &&V_\theta(b,L^{c,D})\\
  &\text{over}
  &&D\subseteq L\setminus\{s_0\}\\
  &\text{subject to}
  &&W(L^{c,D})\le B.
\end{aligned}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
  \max
  \left\{
    V_\theta\bigl(b,(L\setminus D)\cup\{c\}\bigr):
    D\subseteq L\setminus\{s_0\},\
    W\bigl((L\setminus D)\cup\{c\}\bigr)\le B
  \right\}.
\]

Because all active weights are positive and every active incumbent may be
deleted, this conditional problem is feasible exactly when \(w_c\le B\): the
smallest possible post-review library is \(\{s_0,c\}\). If \(w_c>B\), the
candidate cannot be retained under the displayed capacity.

This is a static outer review, not a modification of the existing raw
generation, stochastic verification, admitted-candidate law, or set-union
transition. Mere raw generation gives no selection right. The problem begins
only after \(c\) is independently outer-certified and retention-eligible.
Thus \(c\) is not a transient raw generated-but-unadmitted state in the
existing transition semantics; it is an exogenously eligible candidate
presented to this separate outer review.

The displayed problem is conditional on retaining \(c\). A full accept/reject
problem would compare its optimal value with a separately defined no-\(c\)
outside option. Paper 1 does not silently add that outside option to the
conditional replacement problem.

For the supporting opportunity-cost analysis, define
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \kappa_c(L,B)
    &:=[W(L)+w_c-B]_+,\\
  G_c(b,L)
    &:=
    V_\theta(b,L\cup\{c\})-V_\theta(b,L),\\
  \ell_c(b,L;D)
    &:=
    V_\theta(b,L\cup\{c\})
    -
    V_\theta(b,L^{c,D}).
\end{aligned}
\]
Additivity gives
\[
  W(L^{c,D})\le B
  \iff
  W(D)\ge\kappa_c(L,B).
\]
If \(\ell_c^\star\) is the minimum displacement loss over capacity-feasible
deletions, then
\[
  A_c(b,L,B)
  =
  G_c(b,L)-\ell_c^\star(b,L,B).
\]
The complete definition, safe-release proposition, and accept/reject boundary
are in `REPLACEMENT_OPTIMIZATION_SPEC.md`.

Safety must be indexed by the comparison library. A deletion safe relative to
\(L\) remains safe after inserting the same \(c\), but the converse can fail:
\(c\) may replace the deleted policy's frontier or modules. Therefore failure
of current-library safety does not by itself prove positive replacement loss.
The exact strict-trade-off criterion is absence of a capacity-feasible
zero-loss deletion, or an explicit assumption that every relevant structural
change strictly lowers the stated value objective.

## 7. Inactive strategy and closure conventions

Every feasible or selected library must contain \(s_0\). The inactive
strategy cannot be deleted in \(P_{\mathrm{safe}}\) or the replacement
problem, and it is included in the capacity and penalized domains. Its
resource weight is zero, operational profile is zero, and module set is empty.

The closure is the general finite operator
\[
  \operatorname{cl}:
  \mathcal P_{\mathrm{fin}}(M)
  \longrightarrow
  \mathcal P_{\mathrm{fin}}(M)
\]
that is extensive, monotone, and idempotent. Thus
\[
  C_L
  =
  \operatorname{cl}
  \left(
    \bigcup_{s\in L}\operatorname{mods}(s)
  \right).
\]
Identity closure is an allowed special case, but it is not the Paper 1
default and cannot replace the general closure in theorem statements unless
the result is explicitly specialized.

## 8. Exact finite existence of optimizers

The existence conclusions at the definition level follow from finite,
nonempty feasible sets:

- \(P_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)\) is feasible because \(L\) itself preserves
  \(F_L\) and \(C_L\);
- the dynamic-equivalence form is feasible because
  \(L\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}L\);
- the capacity problem is feasible for every \(B\ge0\) because
  \(\{s_0\}\) has burden zero;
- the penalized problem is feasible because
  \(\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})\) contains \(\{s_0\}\); and
- the conditional replacement problem has an optimizer whenever its feasible
  deletion family is nonempty, equivalently \(w_c\le B\) under the displayed
  unrestricted-deletion convention.

Every domain is a subset of a finite power set. Hence each nonempty domain
attains its minimum or maximum. In the primary finite-horizon layer, all
weights, budgets, prices, productive values, and net values are rational, so
the comparisons are exact. These observations establish mathematical
well-definedness only; they are not yet Lean-verified optimizer-existence
theorems or Julia implementation claims.

Optimizer uniqueness is not implied. Every reported solution must either
return the complete optimizer set or state an explicit tie-breaking rule that
is outside the value definition.

## 9. Four distinct optimization claims

The following terms must remain separate.

| Concept | Exact meaning | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Safe feasibility | \(F_{L'}=F_L\) and \(C_{L'}=C_L\), or equivalently \(L'\sim_{\mathrm{DI}}L\) under the conditions in Section 3 | Minimum burden, deletion irreducibility, or value optimality under scarcity |
| Global optimality | Comparison with every feasible point in the stated domain | Safety unless safety is a constraint; uniqueness; recovery by greedy deletion |
| Dynamic-value optimization | Maximization of \(V_\theta\) under capacity, or \(V_\theta-\lambda W\) under a penalty | Preservation of the source frontier or closure |
| Approximate compression | A separately specified tolerance or loss-budget problem that permits controlled deviations from exact safety | Exact frontier--closure equality, UDI, or a solution to any primary problem above |

Complete rechecked innovation-safe fixed-point deletion supplies a safe,
inclusion-wise irreducible endpoint. Trace safety without an endpoint
completeness condition supplies only feasibility. Neither conclusion certifies
\(P_{\mathrm{safe}}(L)\)'s global minimum. The exact local-versus-global
definitions are in `LOCAL_VS_GLOBAL_COMPRESSION_SPEC.md`.
Conversely, a global capacity or penalty optimizer need not be safe relative
to any current library because safety is absent from those default feasible
sets.

Existing approximate-compression code uses its recorded operational and
generative loss definitions. It must not be reinterpreted as
\(P_{\mathrm{safe}}\), \(V^\star\), or \(J^\star\), and a tolerance result
must not be described as dynamically equivalent without exact verification.

## 10. Constrained versus penalized retention

For fixed \(b,\theta\), the finite eligible libraries generate the attainable
set
\[
  \mathcal A_{b,\theta}
  :=
  \left\{
    \bigl(W(L'),V_\theta(b,L')\bigr):
    L'\in\mathfrak L(S_\theta^{\mathrm{elig}})
  \right\}.
\]
The capacity problem takes the highest value weakly to the left of \(B\). The
penalized problem supports an attainable point with a line of slope
\(\lambda\) in resource--value space.

If \(L_\lambda\) is penalized-optimal at some \(\lambda\ge0\), then it is also
capacity-optimal at budget \(B=W(L_\lambda)\): a library with weakly smaller
burden and strictly larger productive value would contradict penalized
optimality. The converse is false in general. A capacity-optimal attainable
point can be unsupported by every nonnegative price.

### Exact unsupported-point realization

The minimized two-active-strategy Julia fixture has value table
\((0,0,1,3)\) and unit active weights. Its nondominated attainable pairs are
\[
  (W,V)\in\{(0,0),(1,1),(2,3)\}.
\]
At capacity \(B=1\), the library with pair \((1,1)\) is capacity-optimal. For
that library to maximize the penalized objective, it must beat both other
branches:
\[
  1-\lambda\ge0
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \lambda\le1,
\]
and
\[
  1-\lambda\ge3-2\lambda
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \lambda\ge2.
\]
No \(\lambda\ge0\) satisfies both inequalities. The capacity optimum at
\((1,1)\) is therefore skipped by every penalized problem.

The same example has an exact Lagrangian duality gap at \(B=1\). The
capacity value is \(1\), while
\[
  \inf_{\lambda\ge0}
  \max\{\lambda,\ 1,\ 3-\lambda\}
  =
  \frac32.
\]
Thus the dual upper bound exceeds the primal value by \(1/2\).

The complete raw-library table, including the dominated first singleton, is
recorded in
`experiments/results/resource_optimization_fixtures/11_cx_opt_lagrange_unsupported_01.json`.

## 11. Why strong Lagrangian equivalence is not automatic

The library domain is discrete, and productive value can exhibit module
complementarity and other nonconcavities. The attainable
resource--value set need not be convex, and its upper frontier need not be
concave. A penalty price selects only supported attainable points; a hard
budget can select unsupported points such as the exact example above.

Consequently, Paper 1 does not assume:

- that every capacity optimizer is optimal for some \(\lambda\);
- that every budget is implemented by a resource price;
- zero duality gap;
- equality of constrained and penalized optimizer sets;
- differentiability of either value function; or
- a unique optimizer at a budget or price breakpoint.

Strong equivalence would require additional structure, such as an appropriate
convexification, randomized library choice, or a separately proved discrete
integrality/concavity property. None is part of the primary definitions.

## 12. Exact shape results after the search

The exact audit sharpens the four definitions without changing their domains:

- a finite safe optimizer exists, but rechecked pruning need not find a
  minimum-cardinality or minimum-weight optimizer;
- \(V^\star(B;b,\theta)\) is nondecreasing in \(B\), but need not be concave
  and may have increasing unit-budget marginal values;
- \(J^\star(\lambda;b,\theta)\) is a finite convex piecewise-affine envelope
  with a set-valued optimizer at breakpoints;
- penalized-optimal burden is antitone in \(\lambda\), although raw optimizer
  inclusion can switch nonmonotonically;
- an outer-eligible candidate may require capacity-releasing deletion, but
  the deletion does not alter its eligibility status; and
- constrained and penalized optima remain only one-way related without a
  discrete support or convexification assumption.

The exact statements and rejected strengthenings are frozen in
`OPTIMIZATION_THEOREM_REVISIONS.md`. No broader theorem may be formalized from
these computations alone.

## Definition freeze

The following conventions are frozen for the first Paper 1 optimization
layer:

1. exact safe compression is source-library-relative;
2. capacity and penalty optimization use the complete outer-certified
   retention-eligible catalog;
3. mere raw generation does not make a candidate selectable;
4. every library contains the inactive strategy;
5. closure is general, with identity closure only a specialization;
6. the primary productive objective is exact rational finite-horizon value;
7. the replacement problem is a static, conditional-on-retaining-\(c\) outer
   review; and
8. constrained and penalized problems are not presumed strongly equivalent.

Any semantic change requires a superseding decision and synchronized updates
before formalization continues. The current Julia implementation is an exact
outer-library model; no Lean formalization is performed in this change.
