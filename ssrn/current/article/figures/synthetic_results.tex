with 95\% Wilson intervals, in the same adverse (A), null (N), low (L), and

reflect deterministic abstention. The true margins are $-0.027008$, 0,
0.0025, and 0.054016. Common random numbers pair regimes. The powered dose is
chosen by the power design, not estimated from market returns. All plotted
