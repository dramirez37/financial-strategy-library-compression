# V3 claim boundary

## Permitted from the historical development block

- The v3 protocol uses portfolio-level candidates, an always-feasible cash
  option, nested action sets, and an uncertainty-aware adoption rule.
- Exact compression solutions preserve the registered frontier and, for the
  safe arm, the declared executable closure on the audited instances.
- The safe and comparator policies differ in enumerated option coverage,
  frozen proposal decisions, or historical held-out portfolio certainty
  equivalent by the reported finite-panel amounts.
- The historical block repairs or fails to repair specific v2 design
  mechanisms, with every diagnostic labeled retrospective.

## Not permitted from the historical development block

- causal effect of closure;
- population or future-market performance;
- alpha, deployable profitability, or forecasting ability;
- observed institutional savings or maintenance costs;
- proof that declared capabilities reproduce tacit organizational knowledge;
- confirmation independent of the v1/v2 development history; or
- a claim that a favorable robustness specification is the primary result.
- a claim that any registered cost/capacity sensitivity is an executable quote
  or proof of scalable profitability.

## Confirmatory promotion rule

A confirmatory financial claim requires an untouched later vintage or
independent market panel, a source-specific point-in-time contract, a frozen
origin registry, a complete seed and trial ledger, and a design hash sealed
before any confirmatory proposal or evaluation outcome is accessed.
