\section{Full Lean theorem correspondence}\label{supp:lean-correspondence}

The table lists the exact Lean declaration attached to every active
definition, assumption, lemma, proposition, theorem, corollary, example, and
counterexample environment in the final manuscript.  A result without an
exact counterpart is marked explicitly rather than being assigned a
supporting declaration.  The theorem ledger records the corresponding
assumptions and axiom audits.  Candidate Theorems T1--T7 and the active
optimization, capacity, envelope, and elasticity results are included.
Superseded primitive-transition declarations and compatibility aliases are
deliberately excluded; their migration-only record is maintained in the Lean
\texttt{MIGRATION.md} files.
